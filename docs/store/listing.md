## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

> VoicePlatform puts your work phone on your mobile. Make and take calls on your business number, wherever you are, and your desk phone keeps working as it does today.
>
> You need a VoicePlatform account from your workplace to sign in. Your administrator sets it up in the VoicePlatform portal; there is nothing to buy in the app.
>
> **Calls**
> - Calls ring your desk phone and the app together. Answer on whichever is closer.
> - Choose which of your work numbers people see when you call out.
> - Transfer a call to a colleague, talk to them first if you like, or add a third person.
> - Mute, keypad, speaker, hold, Bluetooth and car audio.
> - Calls are encrypted where your workplace's connection supports it, and the screen tells you when they are.
>
> **Voicemail and call history**
> - Play voicemail in the app, call back, and mark it heard. Your desk phone's message light goes off too.
> - Record your voicemail greetings in the app.
> - See who rang, who answered, and whether a missed caller has been called back.
>
> **Colleagues**
> - See who is free, on a call or busy before you transfer.
> - Search colleagues, shared contacts and your phone's own contacts. Your phone contacts stay on your phone.
>
> **Texts**
> - Send and read texts on your workplace's mobile lines, if it has them.
>
> **Your status**
> - Set Do not disturb or Away, and see where your calls go when you don't answer.
> - If you're allowed, open or close the office for the day.
>
> **Emergency calls**
> Calls to 000, 112 and 106 go through your phone's own dialler and mobile network, so emergency services get your location.
>
> **Privacy**
> The app collects only what it needs to work, such as your work email to sign you in, your phone's model and the app version to manage your devices, and crash reports that are cleaned on your phone before they're sent to servers we run ourselves. It never reads your location, and your phone contacts never leave your phone. The privacy policy lists everything.
>
> **Open source**
> VoicePlatform is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3. The source code is at github.com/tangosierra007/voiceplatform-app.
